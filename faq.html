BMS Pro Dial FAQ

Straight answers about the app.

What's a good dial-in app for bracket racing?

BMS Pro Dial is an Android logbook and dial-in app made for bracket racing. You log your passes, and it predicts your ET and suggests a stop from your own runs and the air you're racing in. It works with a stop number, a stop timer, or ET only. Lite is free, so you can try it and decide for yourself.

Does it work for Jr. Dragsters?

Yes. For Jr. Dragsters it suggests ballast pounds instead of a stop. You can keep several cars in the Garage, so Jr. Dragsters and big cars can live in the same app.

Does it work if I don't run a throttle stop?

Yes. Pick ET only, and you get a predicted ET without a stop. Index and ET racers who run without a stop use it that way.

Is BMS Pro Dial on iPhone?

Not right now. BMS Pro Dial is Android only, on Google Play.

Is it free?

Lite is free. Pro is a monthly subscription through Google Play, and first-time subscribers get 2 weeks free. Check the Google Play page for the current price. You can cancel any time in Google Play.

What's the difference between Lite and Pro?

Lite lets you log, edit and export every run, scan timeslips (beta), and use 8 calculators. Lite can use weather from any weather app on your phone. You just type in temp, humidity and baro, and it works out DA and grains. Pro adds auto GPS local weather, auto track weather logged with each run, a 7-day forecast and weather history, the practice tree, and all 26 calculators.

How does it predict my ET and dial-in?

Each car learns from its own logged runs and the weather for each pass, including density altitude and water grains. Every prediction gets a Low, Medium or High confidence label. It's not a guarantee. You still have to drive it.

Where does the weather come from?

Lite can use weather from any weather app on your phone. You just type in temp, humidity and baro, and it works out DA and grains. Pro adds auto GPS local weather and auto track weather logged with each run, pulled from weather services.

Can it read my timeslip?

Yes, with the timeslip scanner (beta), which is in Lite and Pro. Scan the slip or pick a photo of one. The text is read on your phone, and it fills in reaction time, 60 foot, 330, 1/8 mile ET and MPH, 1000 foot, and 1/4 mile ET and MPH. Check Slip shows both lanes side by side, and anything worth a second look gets an amber outline.

Does it work for 1/8 mile and 1/4 mile?

Yes, both.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no app account. Your runs, cars and settings are stored on your phone. You can export your runs as a CSV file whenever you want a backup.

Where is it available?

On Google Play in the US and Canada.
