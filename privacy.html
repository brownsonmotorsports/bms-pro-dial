Privacy summary

This is a short summary of the BMS Pro Dial privacy policy, effective November 9, 2026. The full policy is the one that counts: read the full BMS Pro Dial privacy policy.

  • No account, no server. There's no app account and no Brownson Motorsports server. Your runs, cars, tracks and settings are stored on your phone, and Brownson Motorsports can't see them. If Android backup is on, Android may include the app's data in your Google account backup, which Google manages.
  • Location. Used only if you allow it, when you use weather or elevation features: to get weather, estimate elevation for density altitude, and match you to a nearby track. The app doesn't track your location in the background or record a movement history. You can deny it and enter weather and elevation by hand.
  • Outside services. To get weather and elevation, the app sends coordinates, or a place name you search for, to third-party weather, elevation and place-lookup services. Some past-weather lookups get only a weather station ID and a time range, not your coordinates. All requests use encrypted HTTPS. The full privacy policy names each service.
  • Timeslip scanner. The slip is read on your phone using Google ML Kit. The app doesn't upload slip images or save them with your runs. Only the values you confirm are saved. Google ML Kit may send Google diagnostic information.
  • Pro purchases. Pro is bought through Google Play. Brownson Motorsports never sees your payment details.
  • Export and import. These only happen when you choose to.
  • What we don't do. Brownson Motorsports doesn't sell personal information, doesn't show ads, and doesn't run its own analytics or crash reporting.
  • Children. The app is meant for adult motorsport participants and isn't directed to children under 13.
  • Deleting your data. Your data stays on your phone until you delete it, by deleting runs, cars or tracks, or by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage. Brownson Motorsports keeps no copy to delete.
